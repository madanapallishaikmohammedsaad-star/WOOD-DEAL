# 🪵 Wood Deal

**Deal-by-deal accounting, timber inventory, and partnership management for salvage wood businesses.**

Wood Deal is designed for scrap wood traders, demolition timber businesses, salvage dealers, and auction-based timber trading operations. It treats each purchase, auction, demolition site, or timber lot as a separate profit center, helping users understand the financial performance of each deal without mixing every transaction into one confusing cash-flow pool.

## ✨ Key Features

### 🪵 Deal-by-Deal Accounting
- Create and manage individual deals for timber lots, auctions, scrap purchases, and demolition sites.
- Record deal dates, site locations, target budgets, and statuses.
- Track investment, expenses, sales revenue, and net profit for each deal.
- Keep active, completed, and archived projects organized.

### 🤝 Partner Investments & Profit Sharing
- Record multiple partners and their capital contributions for each deal.
- Configure partner profit-sharing percentages, such as 50/50 or 60/40.
- View partner-wise capital recovery, profit share, return on investment (ROI), and pending payouts as supported by the app.
- Keep deal settlements easier to review and explain to partners.

### 📦 Timber & Stock Inventory
- Record purchased or recovered wood by category, including teak, sal, pine, plywood, demolition beams, and scrap firewood.
- Track quantities using the units supported by the app, such as feet, cubic feet, metric tons, planks, or loads.
- Monitor remaining stock as sales are recorded.
- Connect stock and sales records to the relevant deal.

### 💸 Expenses, Bills & Receipts
- Categorize costs such as labor, loading and unloading, transportation, cutting and sizing, site permits, and equipment rentals.
- Attach and view photos of receipts, expenditure bills, transport bills, and weighbridge slips.
- Keep expense records and supporting documents organized by deal.

### 💰 Sales & Customer Ledgers
- Record sales to wholesale buyers, contractors, and retail customers.
- Associate sales with the relevant deal and stock items.
- Track payments received and outstanding customer balances.

### 📊 Dashboard & Reports
- Review active deals, invested capital, realized revenue, and pending collections.
- View deal-wise profit-and-loss information.
- Review expense breakdowns, margins, and partner settlement summaries where supported.

## 🎯 Who Is It For?
- Scrap wood and salvage traders
- Demolition timber contractors
- Timber auction buyers
- Wood recycling businesses
- Small businesses managing deals with multiple investment partners
